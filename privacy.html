Privacy policy

Conversations · last updated 23 October 2026

Conversations is a private, invitation-only family archive run by one person (“the owner”). This page explains what it collects from visitors and what it does with it.

What the app receives from your Google account

Only your email address. When you sign in, Google asks you to share it; the app uses it to recognise you and check whether you have been given access. It does not receive your name, contacts, Drive files, Gmail or anything else from your account.

What is kept

  • Your access record: your email address, the date you asked for access, any message you wrote with the request, whether it was approved, which parts of the library you may see, and when your access ends.
  • An activity log: when you open the app and which recordings you play, with the date and time. Only the owner can see it.

These are stored in the owner’s own Google account (Google Apps Script), not on any other server.

Emails

When you ask for access, the owner gets an email with your address and message. When access is approved, you get one email saying so. The app sends no other email and no marketing.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. No tracking cookies or analytics are used.

Removing your data

Email suryanaraybehera@gmail.com to have your access record removed. Activity log entries can be cleared at the same time. You can also remove the app’s permission at any time in your Google Account under Security, “Your connections to third-party apps & services”.

Use of Google user data

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
