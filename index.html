💬 Conversations

A private family archive

Conversations is a small private web app where Surya keeps family call recordings and the moments worth remembering from them.

It is not a public service. Access is by invitation only: a visitor signs in with a Google account, asks for access, and the owner decides whether to allow it, which parts to show, and for how long.

Signing in

When you sign in, Google shows a screen named “Conversations” asking to share your email address. That is the only thing the app receives from your Google account. It uses it to recognise you and check whether you have access.

Read the privacy policy for exactly what is kept and why.
